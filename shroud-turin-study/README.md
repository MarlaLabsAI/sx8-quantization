# Shroud of Turin Studies — Estudios de la Sábana Santa de Turín

**Companion study to:** *S-X8 v4.3: A 7.50-Bits-Per-Weight Quantization Format with FP16-Level Quality
and Portable Decoding* — see the paper's **Appendix A** and this folder's
`NOTA_TRANSPARENCIA.md` (ES) / `NOTA_TRANSPARENCIA-EN.md` (EN) for the complete transparency statement.

This folder contains the complete material of an independent mathematical analysis of the image of the
Shroud of Turin (2025–2026): tests, results, re-verifications, visualizations and master documents. It
is published in full, exactly as produced, so that the source of the ideas behind the S-X quantization
methodology is available to anyone who wishes to investigate it.

**Important — read the transparency note first.** The author does not present these analyses as a
veracity, but as a mathematical exercise that served to extrapolate concepts for the quantization
format. Some tests and analyses seem well done; others may be closer, in some way, to pareidolia. The
author cannot corroborate the findings, nor does he intend to: that verification belongs to those with
direct access to the Shroud. The S-X8 format itself has been validated empirically (perplexity and
benchmarks in the paper); this study is disclosed only as the source of inspiration.
